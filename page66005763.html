STAND UP PADDLE EXPERIENCES – MÁNCORA, PERÚ


Explore the sea in a unique way: balance, connection and unforgettable landscapes.

Whether you're looking for a class to learn from scratch, a complete multi-day experience, or simply renting a SUP to explore the waters of Máncora, we have the ideal package for you. All include quality material, professional assistance and photo memories.

Natural bonus:
During our SUP routes, you can visit the sea turtle area, where you can see them up close or swim with them. Additionally, in season (July to October), you have great chances of spotting humpback whales from your board, especially in the morning sessions.

"Explore the sea at your own pace. Live the stand up paddle experience on the beaches of northern Peru."

Classes For all levels

Classes for beginners

Our beginner classes are designed to introduce you to the world of SUP in a safe and fun way. Always under the supervision of experienced instructors.

Advanced Classes with ProRider

If you already have SUP experience and want to take your skills to the next level, we offer advanced classes taught by professional surfers (ProRiders). These sessions focus on advanced techniques, complex maneuvers and detailed analysis of your performance to perfect your style and strategy in the water.
Featured Beaches in Northern Peru
Máncora
Known for its warm waters and consistent waves, ideal for surfers of all levels. In addition, it offers a vibrant nightlife and a wide range of restaurants.
Los Órganos
Quiet beach with waves perfect for those looking for a more relaxed and less crowded atmosphere.
Pocitas
Quiet beach near Máncora, with natural pools at low tide and crystal clear waters, ideal for relaxing.
Vichayito
Perfect spot with constant wind and open sea, away from the hustle and bustle of Máncora.
Punta Ballenas
Not very busy area with impressive views, excellent for hiking and sighting marine fauna.

The Climate in northern Peru


Northern Peru enjoys a warm and sunny climate all year round, with average temperatures between 25°C and 30°C. Conditions are ideal for SUP, especially between the months of April and October, when the waves are most consistent.
Join us and live the Stand Up Paddle experience on the best beaches in northern Peru!
All our classes and SUP trips are subject to sea conditions. Programming may vary depending on swells, tides and wind. Our team will evaluate the best schedules and spots daily to guarantee you the best possible experience in safe and optimal conditions.
