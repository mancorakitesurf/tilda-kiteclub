ABOUT LEARNING KITESURFING
Kitesurfing remains an extreme sport.
Taking kitesurfing lessons is an important step in this regard. This is how you learn step by step to kitesurf safely and responsibly. The teacher will keep up with you and never pressure you if you don't feel ready. In addition, we use safe materials so that we can trust our equipment 100%.
At first glance Máncora is not the easiest place to learn Kitesurfing, however we always recommend learning in conditions like those of Máncora.
Because starting in a lagoon will always be easier, but when you want to kitesurf in other spots you will have to take classes again. While starting with conditions similar to those we have will allow you to navigate anywhere.
The wind in Máncora rises around 11 am until 4/5 pm.
Normally we meet around 12/12:30 depending on the conditions and the beach where we are going to be that day.
For a class we always recommend 2 hours, but if you have energy and if the wind allows us to continue, we can do more class time.
THE BEST TIME TO KITESURF
The wind in Peru is present all year round!
From November to April, you will find good winds south of Lima in Paracas.
From May to December, the windy season is on the northern coast of Peru.
The kitesurfing season in Máncora begins from May to December. We have very good wind conditions in these months with an average of 5 days a week suitable for kite and side-onshore wind.
The wind begins to blow around 11 a.m. and dies at 4/5 p.m.
Máncora is located in front of the intersection of the two large marine currents: the cold Humboldt current (to the south) and the Corriente del Niño (to the north).
The meeting of these two currents creates an excellent climate with more than 300 days of sunshine a year and temperatures that range between 21o and 34ºC and between 20o and 27o for seawater.
The waves in Northern Peru are known for being consistent and diverse, offering great conditions for surfers of all levels. The region has a mix of powerful beach breaks and more mellow waves, depending on the spot. In areas like Máncora, the waves tend to be more manageable and ideal for beginners and intermediate surfers, with nice, long rides. In places like Lobitos and Punta Rocas, you'll find bigger, more powerful waves perfect for experienced surfers looking for a challenge. Overall, the coast is a great spot for both beginners and seasoned surfers, thanks to the consistent swell year-round.
QUESTIONS RELATED TO EQUIPMENT
The most suitable equipment for surfing in Northern Peru depends on your skill level and the wave conditions. Beginners should use a longboard for stability, especially in mellow spots like Máncora. Intermediate surfers can opt for a funboard or mid-length board, ideal for moderate waves in places like Los Órganos and El Ñuro. Advanced surfers will need a shortboard for handling more powerful waves at spots like Lobitos and Punta Rocas. For kitesurfing or wingfoiling, specialized gear like a kite or wing, harness, and board is required, especially in areas with consistent winds like Máncora.
To bring your own kite gear to Peru, check with your airline for their sports equipment policies, as it may count as oversized luggage with extra fees. Pack your gear in a durable, padded kite bag to protect it, disassemble the items for better organization, and consider travel insurance for coverage in case of loss or damage. Ensure you have receipts for customs, though there are generally no issues bringing kiteboarding gear into Peru. Local shops in popular spots like Máncora can help with storage and other needs.
QUESTIONS RELATED TO KITE TRIPS/WINGFOIL
The kitetrips are aimed at an audience with a minimum level of Kite, we ask our clients to have good control of the kite, to know how to rescue themselves and their board.
Kitetrips are not just for surfing, if you sail in a twintip you will also find conditions to have fun.
Yes, Máncora and other beaches in Northern Peru are suitable for practicing wingfoil. Máncora is known for its consistent winds, especially between May and December, making it ideal for wingfoiling. Beaches like Los Órganos and Punta Sal also offer good conditions, with reliable wind and calm waters for beginners and intermediate riders. For more advanced wingfoilers, spots like Lobitos provide stronger winds and bigger waves. Always check the wind forecasts before heading out to ensure the conditions are right for wingfoiling.
THE SCHOOL AND ITS OPERATION
We are located in the center of Máncora, between the main beach (Surfpoint) and Playa del Amor, facing the sea.
It is always best to contact us prior to arrival for our availability, but we do not ask for any booking fees for classes or Kite trips.
Only for accommodation reservations, 50% of the reservation is requested and the other 50% is paid upon arrival at the hotel.
We accept all forms of payment: cash (dollars or soles), payment by card or transfer to bank account (soles and dollars).
If class cannot be done in Máncora, we leave for another nearby beach, without any additional charge.
If we don't find wind on the other beaches either, we plan it for another day, and if you don't have the possibility of postponing the date, we don't charge you anything for missed class hours.
TO ARRIVE
For any country of departure, we recommend that you search for your flights on www.skyscanner.com, it is the easiest site to use and with the best fares.
If you come from Europe, the cheapest option is to leave from Spain.
Once you have chosen your flight, it is always preferable to book your flight on the airline's website.
If you encounter a problem, it will always be easier to communicate with the company's call center than with those on the reservation platforms.
You can choose between 2 options:

The plane: With flight to Piura, Tumbes or Talara.
Piura is 2h30 from Máncora by taxi, Tumbes 1h45 and Talara 1 hour. Through Talara, you will find at least 3 daily flights.

Tips:

If you include stopovers to the final destination from your point of origin, make sure you have enough time for the stopover in Lima (2h30 hours minimum), to have time to clear customs and check-in again without setbacks.

Check with the company if your luggage will go directly to its final destination and that you do not have to pick it up in Lima.

The bus:

The Peruvian bus network is highly developed. There are at least 20 daily departures from Lima to Máncora.
We advise you to choose between Cruz del Sur, Oltursa or Civa (Excluciva or Superciva) since they are the most comfortable and reliable companies.

The safest and fastest way to get to Máncora is with a taxi, you will always find safe taxis at the airport departure gate (we recommend that you never take one outside the airport).

If you need, we can arrange a taxi for you!

If you want a cheaper option, we recommend taking the local bus “EPPO” that connects Piura/Talara with Máncora.

You can take a motorcycle taxi from the airport to the EPPO headquarters (if you come with a lot of luggage, this option will be a little more complicated).

Updated taxi prices are:

- Talara/Máncora: 150 soles
- Tumbes /Máncora: 180 soles
- Piura/Máncora: 280 soles
ACTIVITIES
The good thing about Máncora is that it is a place for everyone, above all it is a place to relax, if you like the sun, the sea and the beach, you will like Máncora.
You can surf, paddle, snorkel, swim with turtles or go whale watching.
Yoga, horse riding, spa, massages, muai thai, etc.
South America is not just beach and surfing, it is also music!
If you want to have a beachfront cocktail for sunset, listen to a live concert or dance reggaeton until dawn, you will find an option for you in Máncora!
We share our recommendations below:

Salsa Thursday in «Psygon». Todo Máncora meets on Thursdays to listen to the band “Máncora All Stars” and dance to their Caribbean rhythms.
Selina: The Selina is open every day, there is no better place to watch the sunset while having a piña colada in front of the sea. Friday and Saturday you will find live concerts, techno, salsa, bachata, there is everything.

And if you prefer to dance reggaeton all night, the resort's "Mareas" and "Terraza" clubs are open every weekend.
Máncora is famous for its food, so near the sea you cannot miss the northern specialties. Here the seafood arrives directly from the sea to the plate.

Our Gourmet tips:

La Sirena de Juan: You can't leave Máncora without having tried a dish at the best restaurant on the north coast. Your Fusion food is an experience you will remember.

The Tao: From Japan through China, Thailand and Peru, the Tao will make you travel with its flavors.

Jasusi: Do you want to try a good ceviche? This typical restaurant in Máncora will make you discover all the specialties of the region.
