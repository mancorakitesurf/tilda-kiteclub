about WINGFOIL

"The trend in water sports".
The first time someone was seen practicing wingfoil was in 2019, when Robby NAish appeared in Hawaii on a board with a foil and handling a wing. Those images caused so much emotion that in a few months we began to see videos and photos on all social networks of different types of wings and people practicing this strange sport. Well, it hasn't been long before we can see the rise of this sport and see countless sails all over the beaches and lakes. Without a doubt a sport that will leave many fans.

WE BROUGHT THE WINGFOIL NORTH

COME FLY WITH US TO "THE CLUB" AND LEARN THIS FASHIONABLE SPORT WITH THE BEST INSTRUCTORS!

  • SIMPLE AND AMAZING

    It is very simple and intuitive, it doesn't matter if you haven't done any sport with wind, you don't have to be an elite athlete to start.

  • the level

    The evolution is incredibly fast. In a few sessions you will be able to make your first controlled flights.

  • UNPARALLELED EXPERIENCE

    The sensation of flying over water is an incredible sensation, you will feel freedom, calm and adrenaline at the same time.

  • EFFICIENCY

    Wing foiling is a super accessible and easy sport to learn. With the right equipment, a good teacher and a few hours in the water you can do the basic maneuvers and start flying.
